## 1. EDUCATIO (umbrella)

**Concept.** Born from summer-2025 board discussions about one "brand" under which all external projects would run (Min. board 11.07.2025: Ramona Nicoară's „Aș vrea să știu" idea; „Rotary talks"; Min. board 27.08.2025). Name „EDUCATIO" (Latin for education) proposed by president Cristian Zaharia on 10.09.2025; kept on 24.09.2025. Under „educație" go all directions: medical, cultural, civic/social, financial, physical/sport, ecological (Min. 10.09.2025). Format: „Dialog educativ" — moderator + 3 invited experts + Q&A, free entry with registration (Plan Social Media: `SRC/EDUCATIO/MediaMind/EDUCATIO_Ed1 - Plan Social Media.docx`). Visual identity by Ovidiu Pop: Σ-logo, one accent colour per direction (`Guideliness.docx`; logos `EDUCATIO-orizontal.png`, `EDUCATIO-vertical.png`).
Official text from the social-media plan (good for site): „Cu EDUCATIO, Rotary pornește o inițiativă dedicată educării continue a publicului atât online, cât și offline… un spațiu deschis pentru dialoguri educative, accesibile tuturor…"
Report: „Lansare proiect EDUCATIO" in Nov 2025. Report and minutes also brand ToyJoy 2025 as „EDUCATIO x ToyJoy ed. a 3-a", and the Olympiad diplomas were framed as Rotary supporting excellence through Educatio (Min. 22.04.2026).
Editions (chronological): MediaMind → MoneyTalks → #GenerațiaFărăHPV → SoundCulture → EcoPulse. File name `EDUCATIO_Ed1` = MediaMind; `rotary/educatio.md` calls MoneyTalks „Ediția 2".
