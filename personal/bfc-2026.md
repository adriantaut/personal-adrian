## Strategy

### Principles (from Sebastian's article)

1. **Body fat % depends on BOTH fat loss AND muscle gain.** Crash dieting alone barely moves the number — you need to preserve/build muscle.
2. **Consistency > extremes.** Sustainable habits win, not 90-day starvation.
3. **Train, eat well, sleep, repeat.**

### My Plan Framework

**Nutrition:**
- [ ] Set daily protein target (~1.6-2.0 g/kg body weight for muscle preservation/gain)
- [ ] Slight caloric deficit (~300-500 kcal/day) — enough to lose fat, not enough to lose muscle
- [ ] Track for first 4 weeks to calibrate (MyFitnessPal / Cronometer)
- [ ] Meal prep on Sundays to remove decision fatigue

**Training:**
- [ ] Resistance training 3-4x/week (compound lifts: squat, deadlift, bench, row, overhead press)
- [ ] Cardio 2-3x/week (Zone 2 for fat oxidation, some HIIT)
- [ ] Consider Padel as active recovery (see Petru / Milan discussions)

**Sleep & Recovery:**
- [ ] 7-8 hours consistent
- [ ] No screens 1h before bed
- [ ] Track with Apple Watch / smart ring

**Metrics to track weekly:**
- Weight (morning, same conditions)
- Waist circumference
- Progress photos (front / side / back)
- Lifting numbers (main compound lifts)
